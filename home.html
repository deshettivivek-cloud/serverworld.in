Strict Examination Integrity

Zero-Cheating Policy

Physical frisking, biometric checks, active invigilation, and CCTV recordings.

Server World implements uncompromising exam integrity protocols across all test centers to ensure 100% fair, transparent, and legally defensible examination results.

🛡️

1. Physical Frisking & Gate Control

Handheld metal detectors and mandatory physical frisking by uniformed security at the entry gate. Zero mobile phones, smartwatches, calculators, Bluetooth devices, or unauthorized papers are permitted into the examination hall.

👤

2. Biometric & ID Verification

Strict verification of Hall Ticket against original Government ID (Aadhaar, Passport, PAN, Voter ID). Digital webcam facial photography and biometric fingerprint enrollment ensure zero impersonation or candidate swapping.

🚶

3. Active Continuous Invigilation

Dedicated invigilators maintain a 1:25 staff-to-student ratio, walking rows every 5 minutes to inspect screens, monitor candidate behavior, verify signature sheets, and ensure zero cross-desk communication.

📹

4. 360° CCTV Surveillance

High-definition 4MP cameras placed at optimal angles cover every seat, aisle, reception, and server room with zero blind spots. Continuous time-stamped recordings with sealed post-exam backups for audit verification.

🔒

5. Secure Isolated Network

Client examination terminals run on an isolated local subnet. Hardware firewalls block internet browsing, social media, messaging apps, USB ports, and remote desktop tools. Tests run in locked-down kiosk mode.

📋

6. Immediate Disqualification & Log

Any attempt at malpractice, whispering, or possessing unauthorized items leads to immediate termination of the test session, incident documentation with exact CCTV timestamps, and formal handover to exam authorities.

⚖️
100% Fair, Transparent & Tamper-Proof Testing
Our Zero-Cheating Policy gives exam boards, government agencies, and candidates complete trust in every score.
